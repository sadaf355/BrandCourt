# Competitor evidence pack — public product positioning

**Captured:** 9 November 2026  
**Method:** Desk research from vendor product pages/support documentation. No competitor trials, demos or hands-on tests were performed. Feature descriptions below are vendor-published claims, not independently verified benchmarks.

## Implications for BrandCourt

1. Do not position generic comments, approval routing, version history or brand guidelines as unique differentiators; established platforms already describe these capabilities.
2. The sharper product hypothesis is evidence-linked claim review: findings cite the approved fact/rule, reviewers can preserve scoped precedents, and a proposed fix is rechecked before being suggested.
3. The differentiator still needs measured proof: false-positive reduction without silencing Critical findings, reviewer time saved, claim-source traceability, and model misses on a genuinely held-out set.
4. BrandCourt should be explicit that it is not a social publisher, real-time social-monitoring service, legal opinion, or substitute for qualified regulatory review.
